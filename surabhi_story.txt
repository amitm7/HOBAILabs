Caption:
"Sandals stitched by hand, second-hand textbooks, Papa driving an auto, and Mom working as househelp, that's where my story begins. I'm Surabhi, an Assamese girl who grew up in a one-room kitchen in Guwahati… with big dreams and no roadmap.
People think struggle feels painful at the moment. But as a child, I didn't even know we were struggling. We had one bed, one table, Papa earned ₹200–₹300 a day driving an auto… But we never felt 'less'.
My dream of becoming an actress first lit up back in 8th grade. I still remember that newspaper ad – Papa held my hand, took me to the audition, I even got selected, but then they asked for ₹1,500 to 'feature' me. Papa gave his hard-earned money, and later, we realised it was a scam. That day, Papa got scared of this industry. By 12th grade, I was juggling life on my own. Teaching kindergarten kids, taking tuitions in the evening, dancing Bihu wherever I got a chance. Somehow, I made ₹5,000 a month and kept going.
College was tougher. Papa couldn't afford my English major fees. A government scheme got me admitted. Seniors gave me old books; I resold them to buy next semester's. Bas isi tarah jugaad karke padhai chalti rahi.
Since 2019, I haven't rested for a single day. From shooting YouTube videos during lockdown to back-to-back music videos, I kept working. I did over 25 music videos, and one of them reached a casting director in Mumbai. Suddenly, I was auditioning for Nima Denzongpa, a big-budget show. I cracked the role. Life changed overnight!
Through it all, one thing never changed, my family. My mother, who made mehendi cones and worked as a househelp; my father, who took me to my first audition… I lost him 3 years ago, and a part of me still wishes he had seen where his Surabhi has reached today. My brother always said, "Tu kar legi," and I wish Papa was around to experience this.
But now, it's my turn to give Maa the life she never asked for but always deserved, to build her a small house in Assam, with an aangan, a mandir, and most importantly, a lot of sukoon…"
