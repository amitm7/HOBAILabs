The point

There is always more story than there is throughput. The bottleneck has never been ideas — it's the hours between a great story and a finished reel. That gap is exactly what we set out to close.

The two problems we feel every week

One slows us down. The other could quietly hurt the brand.

① Volume & speed

Great stories pile up faster than we can cut them. One editor, one timeline, one reel at a time. The backlog grows; our posting rhythm suffers.

② Authenticity & brand risk

Our whole brand is real people, real stories. The moment an AI tool fakes a face or invents a person, we put the one thing we can't replace — trust — at risk.

Most AI video tools are brilliant at speed. Almost none of them are built to protect authenticity. We needed both.

Problem ① · up close

Where the hours actually go

  • A finished story waits in a queue for a free editor.
  • Photos and clips get hunted down and matched to lines by hand.
  • Cutting, captioning, music, voice — one timeline, one reel at a time.
  • Review → revisions → re-export. Repeat per story.

Result: throughput is capped by editor-hours, not by how many stories we have.

The honest math

If a polished reel takes a few focused hours, a small team can only ship so many a week — no matter how many beautiful stories are waiting. Scaling output today means hiring hours. That's slow and expensive.

Problem ② · up close

Our moat is the realness. Don't fake it.

People trust Humans of Bombay because the person on screen is real, and the story is true. That trust is the whole brand.

  • Generic AI video tools generate people. For us, an invented or altered real face isn't a feature — it's a risk to the one thing we can't rebuild.
  • So our rule: real photos and videos pass through untouched. AI fills only the gaps a real asset can't.

Our line in the sand

AI likeness of a named real person is only ever a consented, clearly-labelled exception — never a default. We'd rather animate a real photo than fabricate a human.

The AI video landscape · a fair tour

The tools out there are genuinely good — at their job

Descript

Edit video by editing the transcript; fix audio by typing; Overdub voice cloning; auto-captions & translation. Brilliant for talking-head & podcast edits.

Opus Clip

Turns one long video into many short clips; virality score; auto speaker-framing to 9:16; trendy captions; auto B-roll. Brilliant for repurposing long-form.

Runway (Gen-4)

Text- and image-to-video; consistent characters; native audio; vertical ad templates. Brilliant for generating net-new footage & VFX.

Adobe Premiere

Full manual control, frame-perfect craft, the finishing room. Brilliant for the hero edit and final polish.

How our thinking evolved

We landed on one principle that now guides every call

The value of AI goes up on symbols, sets, motion and assembly — and down the moment it becomes visible on a real human.
