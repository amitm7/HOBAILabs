## What it is
A story -> reel pipeline for **Humans of Bombay (HOB)**: turns a raw story or frame
script + photos/videos into a finished, captioned, scored 9:16 reel (Instagram Reels /
YouTube Shorts). Three front doors on one shared engine: Story `/`, Brand `/brand`,
Studio `/studio`. Hosted on creative.kevat.ai (AWS ap-south-1).

## Audience
- Primary: the HOB editorial/production team (internal creators making story reels).
- Secondary: brand partners doing paid collaborations (Brand mode) - e.g. Tata, AWS.

## Core value proposition
"Best first-draft machine" for emotional story reels: director brain + shot planning +
real-media preservation + brand-compliance, at real throughput. Test-cheap / finish-expensive.

## Positioning / moat
- The moat is **authenticity**: real people, real footage, real trust - NOT the generation
  primitives (those are bought). Value is inversely proportional to how visible the AI is
  on a real human.
- Differentiation vs generic AI video tools: real-media is never AI-regenerated; AI works on
  symbols, environments, motion, assembly, and governance.

## Hard constraints for any marketing output
- No AI-authored ad claims; brand copy is operator-supplied verbatim.
- AI portraits/voices of named real people are a consented, labeled exception, never default.
- Consent + spend-cap gates before paid/external/real-person renders.

## Channels that matter
Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts (9:16 vertical, hook-first, emotional payoff).
